Budget & Glide Path

How the money works (rules updated Sep 12): the buyback budget is $2,000 total for the season, split into two pots on day one. $1,400 is the operating pot — every buyback in Weeks 1–4 comes out of it. The other $600 is locked for Week 5 only — the season's last buyback week, at $225 each, so that pot guarantees up to two revives right before Week 6's must-win no matter how rough Weeks 1–4 were. From Week 6 on there is no buyback at any price — every loss is final. Week 7 was the lone double-pick week (the Week 14 double was cancelled Nov 1) — every entry makes TWO picks and both must win, so those weeks burn roster depth twice as fast; plan hold teams around them. Where we stand (Oct 9): $1,000 in Week 1 + $1,800 in Week 2 + $350 in Week 3 + $3,600 in Week 5 (all 16 Cowboys losses revived, paid for by the Tampa moneyline hedge) = $6,750 in buybacks, $3,600 of it hedge-funded; plus the $600 Week 4 hedge that lost. Buybacks are over: from Week 6 every loss is final. The cap is now a warning at Finalize, not a block; Weeks 3–5 cost $175 / $200 / $225 per rebuy.

Weekly Checklist (before every lock)

  1. Re-check Thursday/Friday injury reports (QBs first: Herbert, Lawrence, Goff, Mahomes).
  2. Check weather (wind first) on outdoor picks — Thursday and again Sunday morning; see the Weather Board.
  3. Re-pull real lines Tuesday — Weeks 2–18 numbers here are projections until lines post.
  4. Re-score the future-value scorecard from look-ahead lines; re-tag hold/spend. Re-score immediately after any major QB injury.
  5. Tie rule: a tie is a loss in this pool (confirmed Sep 11) — treat pick’em-ish spreads with extra caution.
  6. Double-pick week was 7 only — the Week 14 double was cancelled Nov 1 — every entry needs TWO winning teams those weeks; assign the second pick on the weekly board. Buybacks now run through Week 5 ($225).
  7. In buyback weeks, confirm the payment deadline (some pools require payment before Thursday kickoff).

  9. After results: mark Won/Lost above, decide rebuys with the advisor, Finalize Week, then Export state and bring the file to the weekly session.
