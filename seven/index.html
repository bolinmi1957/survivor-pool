Weather Board (who to trust when it's ugly out)

Profile-based — venue, offensive style, and 2025-season form. Mostly a November–January concern: wind of 15+ mph hurts passing offenses more than rain or snow, and cold bites warm-weather and dome teams on the road. Check nflweather.com ↗ Thursday and again Sunday morning before trusting a high win % in an outdoor late-season game.

TierTeamsHow to play it
Built for it Bills, Ravens, Packers, Eagles, Broncos, Patriots, Steelers, Bears, Chiefs, Seahawks Outdoor homes with run games and defenses that travel. Their win % holds up in wind and cold — no haircut needed.
Roof at home Lions, Vikings, Cowboys, Falcons, Saints, Texans, Colts, Raiders, Cardinals, Rams, Chargers Full value indoors. Outdoors from November on, mentally shave 3–5 points off the projected win % — more if wind is 15+ mph.
Fade it Dolphins (cold), Buccaneers & Jaguars (cold road games), any pass-first dome visitor in a wind game Miami's cold-weather road record is a long-running survivor trap. Don't burn a premium team on one of these spots late in the year.
